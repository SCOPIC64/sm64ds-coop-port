A FEW GOOD STARTING POINTS

Make yourself at home.

01

A portable home for your mods.

Drop a Lua mod folder into mods, open Mods and switch it on. Use Refresh Mods after editing. Your selections stay in mods/enabled.txt.

SM64DS Co-op/
├─ sm64ds coop.exe
├─ mods/
│  ├─ sm64-movement/main.lua
│  └─ resource-packs/
│     └─ your-pack/pack.lua
└─ texture-work/capture/

Keep existing saves, settings and edited mods when updating.

02

Give the world your own look.

  1. Open Mods → Texture Pack Tools and start texture capture.
  2. Play the areas you want to edit, then choose Create Pack From Capture.
  3. Edit the PNGs in your new pack’s assets folder. Keep their filenames and restart to load.

Packs live in mods/resource-packs. Each new pack gets its own folder, so your existing work stays intact.

Publish your replacement artwork; keep unedited game captures local.

03

A camera with a familiar feel.

Choose Options → Camera → SM64 Cam. Rotate with Q/E, zoom out with F, or use R for near zoom and C-Up inspection. V switches Lakitu/Mario mode.

Start, Enter or Escape opens the adventure pause menu. Resume, adjust your options, or try another mod.

MADE SOMETHING GOOD?

A little shelf space
for your next idea.

Catalogue submissions are limited to modders approved by the repository owner. Every entry is reviewed before publication; this site has no public upload form.

  1. Get approved. Ask the maintainer to add your GitHub username to the approved-modder list.
  2. Prepare your release. Include the DS API version, installation folder, source and download link. Host your work on GitHub.
  3. Submit for review. Add your entry to the catalogue in a pull request. Approval grants submission access; it doesn’t publish automatically.
