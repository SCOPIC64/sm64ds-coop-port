#  three. Bowser::InitResources spawns 0x118 (280) and 0x116 (278) and writes

#  faults on his first init frame. Verified in src, not inherited.

#  BOWSER SKY PLATFORM (167, daKpa3Bg_c) -- the koopa3 arena's floor. Its
#  vtable is the UNNAMED data_ov060_0211a9b0 (the other half of the label
